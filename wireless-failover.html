FAQ

Wireless Failover FAQs

What is wireless failover, and does my restaurant need it?

It’s an automatic backup internet connection that activates when your main network fails. If you rely on VoIP phones, online ordering or a POS, downtime means lost revenue.

How fast does the switch happen?

Virtually instantly. Calls and data reroute through 4G/5G within seconds, so customers don’t hit busy signals or error pages.

Does it only protect my phones, or online ordering too?

Both. It covers VoIP phones, online ordering, your branded app, POS systems and delivery coordination at the same time.

Is it complicated to set up?

No. It works with your current systems, needs no complex configuration, and our team handles the installation.

Do I have to switch back manually?

No. Switching back is automatic, with no rebooting, IT calls or staff action.
