FAQ

Online Ordering FAQs

How is this different from a third-party delivery app?

Third-party apps take a big cut of every order and control your customer data. Ours is a white-label platform on your own domain, so the revenue and the data go straight to you.

How long does it take to launch a branded ordering app?

Most restaurants go live within a few weeks. We handle design, menu setup, POS integration and app store submission, and you review and approve.

Can customers reorder without starting from scratch?

Yes. One-tap reordering is built in, with order history and notifications when favorites or promotions come back.

Does it integrate with my existing POS?

It supports many major POS platforms. Onboarding covers menu structure, modifiers, routing and kitchen display setup.

Can I run promotions and limited-time offers?

Yes. Manage promotions, item visibility, pricing windows and push notification campaigns from one dashboard.

Does it work for multiple locations?

Yes. Manage menus, pricing and promotions at the brand level or per location, with unified reporting.
