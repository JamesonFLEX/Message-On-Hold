FAQ

Delivery Logistics FAQs

What makes Message On Hold different from other delivery logistics software?

Many platforms hand you tools and leave setup to you. We pair the software with ongoing account support to configure, optimize and grow your operation.

Can it handle in-house drivers and third-party couriers?

Yes. Dispatch to your own drivers, our courier network or third-party providers from one dashboard, and the platform picks the best option by volume, location and delivery window.

How does the POS integration work?

Phone, online and in-store orders flow into the delivery queue without manual entry. We handle the integration during onboarding and set up routing to fit your kitchen.

Is it suitable for a single-location restaurant?

Yes. Single-location operators get dispatch, routing and customer notification tools that used to be enterprise-only.

What if a delivery issue happens after hours?

Our US-based support team is available 24/7 for driver issues, customer complaints and system questions.
