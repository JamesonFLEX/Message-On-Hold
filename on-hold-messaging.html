FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long should on-hold messaging be?

We match the length to how long your callers typically wait, so the message flows naturally without repeating too often. Our team will recommend a length for your store.

Can messages be updated?

Yes. You can update your message at any time, whenever your specials or promotions change.

Will this work with my phone system?

Our team helps with setup and will check compatibility with your phone system before you start.

Do I need to write the script myself?

No. You tell us what you want to promote, and we write and record the script for you.

How quickly can I get on-hold messaging up and running?

Once you’ve chosen your promotions, we write, record and help you set it up. Ask about current turnaround times; rush production is available.
