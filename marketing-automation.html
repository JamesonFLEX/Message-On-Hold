FAQ

Marketing Automation FAQs

How does marketing automation work in practice?

Each completed order can trigger a review request, loyalty points, a follow-up offer or a reorder reminder, based on the customer’s profile and behavior. You set the rules once and the platform runs them at scale.

How soon will we see results?

Reviews and repeat orders build as the automations run, loyalty grows over time, and targeting gets sharper as customer data builds up.

Can I control what messages go out and when?

Yes. You set send windows, message content, offer values and trigger conditions, and our team helps during onboarding and with ongoing adjustments.

Does the Google Review Booster violate Google’s review policies?

No. Requests go to real customers at natural moments, without incentives, to gather authentic feedback.

Do I own my customer data?

Yes. The platform collects and organizes it for you, and you can keep, export and use it as you need.
